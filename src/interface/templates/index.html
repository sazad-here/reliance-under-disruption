Warehouse Ordering Study

Decision-making with an AI forecasting assistant

You will manage a warehouse for 30 weeks. Each week you see the current stock position and decide how many units to order. An AI assistant will offer a recommendation, but you decide.

On every week you will be asked twice:

  • first for your own order, before the assistant's advice appears;
  • then for your final order, after you have seen it.

Five practice weeks come first. The session takes about 45–60 minutes, including short questionnaires after every ten weeks. Taking part is voluntary and unpaid.

Read the participant information sheet (opens in a new tab). Please read it before you consent.

Use the ID shown in SONA, or any code you will remember (3–32 letters or numbers).

Enter your participant ID and tick the box to continue.
