Participant Information Sheet

Warehouse Ordering Study: decision-making with an AI forecasting assistant

Version 1.1 · Ethics reference [ETHICS REF]

You are invited to take part in a research study. Please read this sheet before deciding. You can ask questions at any point using the contact details at the end.

What is the study about?

Organisations increasingly use AI tools to help people make operational decisions. We are studying how people make repeated ordering decisions for a warehouse when an AI assistant offers recommendations, and how those decisions play out over time.

What will I do?

  • Answer three short arithmetic questions. These check the task will make sense to you. If the answers suggest it may not, the study ends there.
  • Manage a simulated warehouse for 5 practice weeks and then 30 weeks. Each week you enter your own order, then see the AI assistant's recommendation, then enter your final order.
  • Answer short questionnaires about the assistant and your workload after every ten weeks, and one question at the end.

It takes about 45–60 minutes. Taking part is voluntary and unpaid. Participants are recruited through the SONA participant pool at [INSTITUTION].

Is there anything I should know about the assistant?

The assistant will not always be right, just as real decision tools are not. You are free to follow it, change it or ignore it. Some details of how the assistant works are explained only at the end of the session, because knowing them in advance would change how people use it. Nothing you are told during the study is false, and a full explanation is given before you leave.

Are there any risks or benefits?

There are no known risks beyond those of everyday computer use. Some people find the task mildly frustrating when the assistant's advice turns out badly. There is no direct benefit to you. The results will help design decision-support tools that people can rely on appropriately.

What data is collected?

  • Your orders, response times, questionnaire answers and the simulated outcomes.
  • A participant ID: the ID shown in SONA, or a code you choose. It is used only to find your answers if you ask for them to be deleted, and it is removed [RETENTION PERIOD, e.g. when data is anonymised].
  • No name, email address, location or other identifying information is collected.

Data is stored securely by [INSTITUTION] and kept for [RETENTION PERIOD FOR RESEARCH DATA]. An anonymised version, with no participant IDs, may be published so other researchers can check the results.

Do I have to take part? Can I withdraw?

Taking part is voluntary and unpaid. You can stop at any time without giving a reason, and stopping has no consequences for you:

  • During the task, use “Withdraw from the study” at the bottom of the page. You can choose to have everything you have entered deleted immediately.
  • At the end, after the explanation, you can again choose to delete your data.
  • Later, message the researcher with your participant ID before [DATE, e.g. when data is anonymised], and your data will be deleted. After that date it cannot be identified and removed.
